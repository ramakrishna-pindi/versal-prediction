SAVE ENERGY

How to Reduce Your Electricity Bill

These are general precautions. Actual savings depend on appliance efficiency, tariff, usage and local electricity rules.

💡

Lighting

Use LED bulbs, daylight and switch off lights in empty rooms.

❄️

Air Conditioner

Use a suitable temperature, clean filters and keep doors/windows closed while running.

🧊

Refrigerator

Avoid unnecessary door opening and keep seals and temperature settings in good condition.

🌀

Fans

Switch off fans in empty rooms and keep blades clean.

📺

Electronics

Switch off unused TVs, chargers and electronics instead of leaving them unnecessarily powered.

🧺

Washing Machine

Prefer suitable full loads and efficient cycles.

☀️

Natural Energy

Use daylight and natural ventilation when practical.

🌱

Track Usage

Compare monthly units and investigate sudden increases in consumption.
